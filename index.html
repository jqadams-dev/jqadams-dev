01. About Me

The Engineer Behind the Network

I'm J.Q. Adams — a Network and Unified Communications Engineer with over two decades of hands-on experience designing, implementing, and maintaining enterprise-grade infrastructure. I'm based in Headland, Alabama, and I work with clients both locally in Southeast Alabama and remotely across the country.

My background spans Cisco networking and UC engineering — a combination that's rarer than most people realize. While many engineers specialize in one or the other, I've spent years working across both disciplines in large-scale, complex environments where reliability isn't optional.

What drives me right now is the intersection of traditional networking and modern automation. I'm actively building skills in Python and AI — not as a hobby, but as a deliberate move toward the future of network engineering. Networks are getting smarter. I intend to be ahead of that curve.

When I'm not engineering infrastructure, I'm studying, labbing, and building — always with one eye on where networking is heading next.
